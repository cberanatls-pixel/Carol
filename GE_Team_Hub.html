Callouts this week Please take note 👇
Always End with a Question or Follow-Up
Every email and message should close with a question or a next step. Don't leave the guest hanging — keep the conversation moving.
FI Must Be Clear and Accurate
The FI is not just for the guest — the villa team reads it too. Make sure it is clear, accurate and easy to understand by everyone.
Avoid Guest Follow-Ups
Add pending items to CRM tasks and notate accordingly. If it is in the system — it will not be forgotten. No excuses.
