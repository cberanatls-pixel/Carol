Callouts & Reminders Please take note 👇
Set the Expectation — Even If Not Confirmed
Guest mentioned something that could mean an extra cost — additional rooms, early check-in, special requests? Address it now. Don't wait for it to become a surprise.
"Noted" Is Not Enough — Assist
Acknowledging is the start, not the finish. Read the situation. Act on it. Close the loop properly.
CRM Tasks — Know It by Heart
Open the ITI. Open the CRM. Add the task. Every single assist. This should be second nature by now.
Cover for Your Teammates
Someone is out or on RD? Check their tasks. Assist where you can. We are one team — zones don't matter here.
Re-Read. If It's Confusing — Fix It
If your own message confuses you, it will confuse the guest. Don't send it. Rewrite it until it is clear and accurate.
