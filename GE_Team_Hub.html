Callouts & Reminders Please take note 👇
Response Time — Assist, Don't Just Acknowledge
Responding is the start. Assisting is the job. Make sure every reply moves things forward.
Sense of Urgency — Take Ownership
Arrival in 5 days and the GE is on off? Don't wait. Step in. Own it. That is what the team does.
Teamwork — Help Beyond Your Zone
Your guests are not the only ones that matter. Look out for your teammates. Help where you can.
Processes Are a Guide — Not the Ceiling
Use the process as a foundation. But think. Read the situation. Do what is right for the guest.
Read Between the Lines
If something feels off — address it. Set the expectation. Offer alternatives. Don't wait for the guest to raise it.
Say It — But Say It Well
Draft with care. Offer options. Even hard news can be delivered well — and that is what separates a good GE from a great one.
CRM Tasks — Do Them. Don't Just Close Them.
Complete what is assigned on the day. Closing without acting means the guest doesn't get updated — and a guest who follows up is the last thing we want.
