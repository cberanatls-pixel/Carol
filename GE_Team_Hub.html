Callouts this week August recurring themes — let's make September different 👇
Personalization
Every guest is different. Read the booking, know the context, and write like you mean it. Generic emails have no place here.
Response Time
Guests should not be waiting. Reply within your shift window — no exceptions, no excuses.
FI Accuracy
The FI must be complete, accurate, and reflect every request. If it is not in the FI — it did not happen.
Check Resources First
Before you ask — check. The files, the tools, the providers. The answer is usually already there.
Guest Follow-Up
If a guest is following up — we were too slow. Update proactively. Close the loop before they have to ask.
Set Expectations
Always tell the guest what to expect and when. No surprises. No assumptions. Clear communication every time.
