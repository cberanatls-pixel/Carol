Callouts this week Please take note 👇
Response Time
Guests should not be the ones following up. Reply within the required window — every time, no exceptions.
Guest Follow-Up — Big No
A follow-up from a guest means we dropped the ball. Update proactively. Own the conversation before they ask.
Don't Just Acknowledge — Handle It
Saying "noted" is not enough. Read the situation and take action. Resolve it. Follow through. Close the loop.
Put Requests in FI
Every guest request goes in the FI — no exceptions. If it's not there, it didn't happen.
Set Expectations
Guests should always know what to expect and when. If there's a delay or process — tell them upfront. No surprises.
GE team updates
  • Guest Tracker Notes — Log what you did on each booking directly in the guest tracker. Format: 8/14/2026 CB — GRO to provide transfers | updated ITI | ffd up on meals. Use your initials for ownership.
  • Frustrations & Complaints — Raise to management immediately. If no manager is available, acknowledge the guest and provide a clear timeframe for when one will be.
  • Escalations — Always include Villa Name · Guest Name · Arrival Date when raising questions, approvals or concerns.
