Callouts & Reminders Please take note 👇
Lock the ITI Before Sending the FI
The guest should receive a final version — not a working draft. Lock the ITI link before the FI goes out.
When There Is a Charge — Explain It
Don't just send a rate. Tell the guest what it covers and why. It's an easier conversation now than at check-in.
Before You Hit Send — Read It Again
Right guest. Right message. Right tone. One re-read before sending saves a lot of fixing after.
Acknowledge — Even Without the Answer
No answer yet? That's fine — but let the guest know you are on it. Don't leave them wondering.
Say It — But Say It Well
When delivering something the guest may not want to hear, draft it with care — and always offer options. The goal is to inform, not to close the door.
Call Before Sending the FI
A quick call sets expectations and catches last-minute changes. No answer? Note it, proceed, and make sure the FI is complete.
